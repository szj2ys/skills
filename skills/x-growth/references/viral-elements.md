# Viral Elements

Seven elements that make an audience want to engage. Originated from the user's own content framework. Use them as both an **analysis lens** (why did this post work) and a **generation checklist** (which element does my draft hit). A piece of content or a reply should trigger at least one; the strongest hit two or three at once.

## The 7 elements

1. **观众想纠正你 — They want to correct you**
   - Post a slightly-wrong or oversimplified take; experts rush to fix it.
   - Reply use: offer a correction or nuance.
   - Trigger phrase: "Isn't X just Y?" (when it isn't).

2. **观众想看结果 — They want to see results**
   - Show real numbers / outcomes, not theory.
   - Reply use: ask for the underlying detail behind the number.
   - Trigger phrase: share a concrete metric + what it meant.

3. **观众想看翻车 — They want to see the failure**
   - Real setbacks beat success porn. Vulnerability earns trust.
   - Reply use: share your own failure story.
   - Trigger phrase: "I shipped X and it bombed because…"

4. **观众想辨真伪 — They want to verify truth**
   - Make a counterintuitive or bold claim that invites proof/disproof.
   - Reply use: challenge or corroborate with evidence.
   - Trigger phrase: "Everyone says X. I think the opposite, because…"

5. **观众想给你支招 — They want to give you advice**
   - Ask for help on a real, specific problem.
   - Reply use: ask a sharp follow-up question.
   - Trigger phrase: "Stuck on X, what would you do?"

6. **观众想带入自己 — They want to project themselves**
   - Relatability: "this is exactly where I am."
   - Reply use: "I'm in the same spot, here's my version."
   - Trigger phrase: describe a stage/struggle the reader recognizes.

7. **观众想证明自己 — They want to prove themselves**
   - Let them show expertise.
   - Reply use: ask them to elaborate their method.
   - Trigger phrase: "What's your playbook for X?"

## Mapping table

| Element | Best for replies | Best for posts |
|---|---|---|
| Correct you | nuance / pushback | bold simplified claim |
| See results | ask for the driver | share real metric |
| See failure | share your flop | tell the flop story |
| Verify truth | challenge / evidence | counterintuitive claim |
| Give advice | sharp question | ask for help |
| Project self | "same here" + your version | relatable struggle |
| Prove self | ask for their method | open-ended expertise ask |

## Diagnostic use
When analyzing a target post, label which element(s) it triggers, then write the reply to **amplify** that element. Example: a post about a launch getting throttled triggers "see failure" → reply with your own throttling story (element 3) and a question (element 5). Two elements, one reply.
