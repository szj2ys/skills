## How to use
- Log **every original post AND every meaningful reply** — especially replies that got a like or reply from the original poster (OP). Those are your highest-leverage moments.
- After 7 days, ask: which **Type / Theme** has the highest *profile visits per impression*? Do more of that. Which flopped? Swap the hook.
- **Followers gained** is the lagging metric; **profile visits** is the leading one. Watch visits first — followers follow visits.
- If a reply got an OP reply/like, note the exact phrasing that worked so it can be reused as a template.
